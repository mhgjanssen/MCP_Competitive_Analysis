## 1. Executive Summary

- Reltio pushed the 2026.1.10.0 bi-weekly release to production today (Sept 11); the AgentFlow update focuses on collaboration and observability of MCP-grounded agents rather than net-new MCP surface.
- Akeneo published its first primary-source press release since the July 8 Summer 2026 launch — a Sept 9 "Agentic Commerce Reality Check" survey (1,000 senior IT decision-makers) reframing product data, integrations and governance as the actual bottleneck to agentic commerce.

## 2. Key Updates This Week

### Reltio — Updated

Summary: Reltio 2026.1.10.0 went to production Sept 11. AgentFlow adds shareable revocable read-only links to conversations; interactive charts/diagrams on by default for custom and long-running agents; email notifications on task start/stop/complete/fail; and a Job Result view with per-record status, confidence, reason, outcome and full conversation trace. The release also expands Reltio Data Sharing with Databricks (additional object-level properties) and formalises August 2027 deprecation of legacy RDM endpoints.

Why it matters: Reltio is optimising the day-2 experience of MCP-grounded agents (auditability, collaboration, notification) rather than adding MCP endpoints — a signal that deployments are moving from build to run.

### Akeneo — New

Summary: On Sept 9 Akeneo issued a primary-source release, "AI Ambition Is Outpacing the Commerce Foundations Needed to Deliver It," accompanying an Agentic Commerce Reality Check report (1,000 senior IT decision-makers, U.S. and Europe). 95% globally are confident their organisation is ready for AI-driven commerce and 87% expect AI budgets to rise, yet product-data quality, integration and governance are cited as the operational barriers to actually delivering it. Trade press pickup Sept 10 highlights "ten hard truths."

Why it matters: MCP-adjacent. Akeneo's clearest attempt yet to own the "AI-readiness = governed product data" narrative on top of its existing MCP Server + Agentic Ziggy stack, placing it in the same governance-for-agents framing as Informatica, Reltio and Syndigo.
