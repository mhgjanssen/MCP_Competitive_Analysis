Summary: Oct 7, 2026 resource “How to solve your AI content control problem once and for all” publishes a four-measure framework for governing AI-generated product content: (1) limit what the AI can change, (2) assign a decision owner, (3) add a verification step against trusted data, (4) keep an approval log before publication. MCP-adjacent — speaks to AI governance for product data without naming a specific protocol.

3. Notable Patterns

  • “Verified MCP servers only” becomes an explicit SAP policy. SAP AI Agent Hub GA (Oct 1) restricts production invocation to MCP servers that pass a formal verification workflow and automatically revokes access when verification status changes. First large-vendor runtime gate on MCP in the tracked set — reframes “MCP support” as a governance and identity question. Pimcore's 2026.3 native OAuth 2.0 server and Informatica's Secure MCP Gateway are the vendor-side analogues.
  • SAP's two-SKU MDM story continues to productise. Reltio 2026.1.12.0 adds the 5th SAP-branded Velocity Pack (Banking) one week after the first four. The “SAP MDG at source + SAP Reltio for enterprise unification” architecture codified Sept 30 is now backed by weekly content releases, not just positioning.
  • Fabric OneLake is becoming a competitive battleground for governed MDM. Profisee Anywhere joins Reltio (Databricks Zero-Copy), Pimcore Embeddings, SAP Reltio (SAP BDC) and Informatica (Headless Data Management across every major cloud) in a cross-vendor race to put MDM/PIM state inside the customer's primary analytics / AI data store rather than beside it — governance posture matters because the data no longer moves.
