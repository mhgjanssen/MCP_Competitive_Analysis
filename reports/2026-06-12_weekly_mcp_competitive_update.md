3. Notable Patterns
- Vendor M&A is becoming an explicit agentic-commerce play: Akeneo + PricingHUB (this week), SAP + Reltio (closed May 7), Salesforce + Informatica (closed November). PIM/MDM vendors are buying their way into adjacent layers (price, governance, integration) on the explicit logic that AI agents need a unified decision layer, not isolated capabilities.
- "Agentic" is now appearing in vendor self-descriptions, not just product descriptions — Akeneo's "Agentic First Product Cloud … Agentic by design. Driven by governance." this week mirrors Pimcore's "Agentic PXM," Syndigo's "Agentic PXM," Salsify's "agentic shelf" and Informatica's "autonomous data management agents." The category vocabulary is consolidating.
- Explicit MCP support is moving from roadmap line item to public marketing claim — Inriver's competitive-landscape page (June 10) and Akeneo's homepage ("APIs, MCP, and full custom extensibility included") both call out MCP by name. The shift from "we will support MCP" to "we already support MCP, and our competitors do/don't" is starting to harden.
