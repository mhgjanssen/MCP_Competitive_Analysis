3. Notable Patterns
- Vendors are starting to monetise the agentic-commerce thesis on the demand side, not just the data-operations side. Syndigo's Conversion Framework and Salsify's three-shelf holiday research both translate "AI agents will read your product data" into a conversion / revenue story aimed at brand and retail buyers.
- Governance-framework extensibility is becoming a stealth competitive axis underneath the MCP headline. SAP MDG's cloud-ready Custom Objects and Pimcore's Data Spine + Agent SDK both let customers add governed domains without breaking the clean-core / agent-ready model — the architectural prerequisite for "everything MDM exposes can also be MCP-exposed."
- Three distinct messaging axes are now visible: Informatica leading on cross-cloud, cross-platform MCP breadth; SAP/Reltio on agent governance and observability inside one ecosystem (SAP BDC + Joule + A2A); and PIM/PXM vendors (Syndigo, Salsify, Akeneo, Inriver) diverging into commerce-outcome framing.
