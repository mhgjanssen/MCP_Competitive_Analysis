3. Notable Patterns
- The competitive narrative is consolidating around three explicit layers: (1) MDM / governance exposed as MCP servers, (2) autonomous agents (data quality, stewardship, enrichment) operating on top, (3) a unified agent + context catalog as the governance control plane. Informatica is currently the most complete public articulation of all three.
- "Closing the AI governance gap" is becoming the dominant business framing — survey-led storytelling (e.g. 76% governance gap, 61% data-quality blocker) is replacing pure capability messaging, raising the table-stakes for governance-over-agents positioning.
