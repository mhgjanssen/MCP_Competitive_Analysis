3. Notable Patterns
- The SAP-anchored MDM/PIM landscape is visibly reshuffling: Reltio is now inside Business Data Cloud, Inriver is doubling down as the SAP-certified best-of-breed PIM, and SAP itself did not surface a refreshed MDG roadmap at Sapphire 2026 — a clear competitive window for SAP-coexistence MDM-for-agents stories.
- Vendors are pivoting from "we have AI agents" to "we govern AI agents": SAP AI Agent Hub bundling, Informatica's Agent Fabric Context Catalog and Pimcore's identity/permissions framing are all moving agent governance into a competitive axis of its own.
- For PIM-side vendors, the narrative has shifted from "AI features" to "trusted governed product data feeding agentic commerce" — with MCP increasingly cited as the standard interface, even where vendor MCP support is still partial.
